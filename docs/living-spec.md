## By area

### Ask Alcan (super admins only, for now)

- Ask a question in plain words and get an answer drawn from Alcan's own
  documents, with links to the documents it used. Conversations are private
  to the person asking; they can delete them.
- Rate any answer thumbs up or thumbs down. After a thumbs down, add an
  optional short note about what was off. Tap the same thumb again to clear
  the rating; switching to thumbs up clears the note.
- Rating an answer shares that one question and answer with the Ask Alcan
  team, and the screen says so. Nothing else from the chat is shared.
- In Admin, on the Ask Alcan tab, super admins see "Answer feedback": every
  rated answer, newest first, with its question, the note, and the documents
  it cited, filterable by thumbs up or down. It never shows who asked. A
  cleared rating or a deleted chat drops off the list.

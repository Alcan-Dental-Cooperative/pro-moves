# The Avenue patient flow, old and new: poster content draft

**What this is:** the staff-facing process map for the training day, drafted in
text for John's check before it becomes a visual. Two panels side by side: the
old world on the left, deliberately muted, and the new world on the right,
colour-coded by owner. Everything in quotes or numbered lists below is literal
poster text. *Italic lines are layout notes, not poster text.*

Sources: journey doc v0.3 (`docs/alcan-avenue-patient-journey.md`), condensed
hard. A wall poster carries moments, not standards; the full detail lives in
the journey doc and the JDs.

---

## Panel 1 (left): TODAY

### "How it works today: whoever is free"

*A single grey path, slightly tangled on purpose. No colour coding, because no
one owns anything.*

1. **An enquiry arrives.** Phone, website, Facebook, a walk-in.
2. **Whoever picks up owns that moment.** Usually reception, mid-task.
3. **Answered:** quick answers, sometimes a price on the phone.
   **Missed, or no reply:** one try, then nothing. There is no second attempt.
4. **On CareStack only if they book.**
   *Callout box:* **61 of every 100 people we actually spoke to never made it
   onto the system.**
5. **They arrive.** Whoever is at the desk greets them.
6. **The dentist does everything:** diagnosis, the plan, and the money
   conversation.
   *Callout box:* **the £49 exam becomes a £3,000 surprise.**
7. **Hygiene gets a mention at the end,** if there is time. The patient is
   left to rebook it.
8. **"We'll be in touch."** Nobody's name on it.
9. **Recall lives on a dentist's private list.**
10. **A complaint goes to whoever the patient can find.**

*Bottom caption, full width:*
> **Nobody owns the patient. A record is not a relationship.**

## Panel 2 (right): FROM 6 OCTOBER

### "One named owner, three clear paths"

*Colour-coded by owner: TCO, Reception, Therapist, Nurse and Dentist, plus a
neutral colour for shared rules. Legend in the corner.*

**Top box, before the split:**
> **Every new enquiry goes to the Treatment Coordinator.** When the TCO is
> with a patient, reception catches it: acknowledged within 10 minutes
> (20 at the very worst), and the real callback comes within the hour.

**The triage question:** *"What brings them in?"*
- Thinking about a treatment → **Track A**
- Joining the practice → **Track B**
- Already our patient → **Track C** *(slim side lane, see below)*

### Track A: the treatment journey *(owner: TCO, end to end)*

1. **The enquiry lands.** On CareStack from the very first call.
   *Loop arrow on this step:* **No answer? Five attempts over ten days, then
   closed with a reason. Never silently dropped.**
2. **The first call is discovery, not prices.** What is going on, why now,
   why us. The consultation gets booked, with a £35 refundable deposit.
3. **The consultation.** Free, with the TCO: scans, photos, and your story.
4. **Findings, and a ballpark.** What we found, and roughly what it costs,
   today. No surprises later.
5. **Ready?** The clinical exam gets booked. **Not yet?** A review with a
   real date in the diary. Never "we'll be in touch".
6. **Exam day.** The TCO greets you, hands you warmly to the nurse and
   dentist, confers with the dentist on the plan, and sees you again before
   you leave the building.
7. **Treatment**, with a day-after call for the big procedures.
8. **Treatment ends.** Into routine hygiene and the one shared recall
   pipeline.

### Track B: the joining journey *(owners: therapist first, then TCO)*

1. **The joining call.** A hygiene visit is booked first, for every new
   patient.
2. **The hygiene visit.** A proper clean and real oral health education. The
   therapist is the front door of the practice.
3. **Found something worth a conversation?** The TCO is introduced in the
   room, there and then, while it is still on the screen.
4. **Then the dentist.** The patient arrives already cleaned and already
   informed.

### Track C: our established patients *(owner: reception)*

*Kept to one small box so existing patients see themselves on the map:*
> Front of house, confirmations, checkout, reviews: as today, done well.
> Recall comes from the shared pipeline, not from private lists.

### Bottom strip, full width: "True everywhere"

- Every new patient has **one named owner** with a direct number.
- **Prices are never quoted on the phone.** The ballpark comes at the
  consultation.
- **Dentists keep their chair time.** The money conversation belongs to the
  TCO.
- **Every complaint:** restate it, "tell me more", agree the next step. The
  practice manager always knows.

## Layout notes for the artifact

- Landscape, the two panels equal width, a bold dividing line between the
  worlds. Works on a screen for the training day and prints to A3 for a wall.
- Old panel: greyscale, the path drawn as one slightly wandering line, the
  two callouts as torn-edge boxes.
- New panel: clean vertical tracks A and B side by side under the shared
  triage box, Track C as a slim lane on the far right, the "true everywhere"
  strip anchoring the bottom.
- Owner colours double as the same colours used on role-play casting cards
  on the day, so the mapping sticks.

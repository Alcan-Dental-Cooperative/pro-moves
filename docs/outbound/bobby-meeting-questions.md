# Nine questions for Bobby

*One page for the meeting. Each question notes why it matters now and where it
comes from; the source key is at the bottom. Answers feed three things: the
training day casting sheet, the shape of the day itself, and the fortnight of
structural switches after it.*

**1. The practice manager: start date, and are they in post before 6 October?**
The day deliberately installs the PM's authority (they close the commitments
session); an empty chair undermines it, so we need to know which script to run.
*Source: [REV-CALL], two candidates, roughly four weeks' notice.*

**2. Millie: separate her skill-readiness from her hours.** Is the Monday/Friday
part-time TCO cover hire decided (open question 12 from the review)? Friday is
the second-quietest day, so Monday-weighted cover would suffice.
*Sources: [REV-CALL] Q2 and open Q12; [CU] for the Friday figure.*

**3. Angelene: ready to run reception solo, plus the new catch duty** (acknowledge
enquiries within 10/20 when the TCO is busy)? And where does Abbie fit?
Determines the reception casting in the enquiry role-plays.
*Sources: [REV-CALL]; catch duty defined in [TCO-NARR].*

**4. The nurse roster: names, and who is newest.** A nurse started in early
August; casting needs to know who plays staff roles and who plays patients
(newest in as a patient first).
*Source: [REV-CALL].*

**5. Is the practice fully closed on 6 October?** The date itself is settled and
optimal (Tuesday is the quiet day, 7.35 patients/day against Wednesday's 9.46);
full versus partial closure decides a full-day or compressed agenda.
*Source: [CU].*

**6. Is the new therapist attending?** They lead Track B and carry the hygiene
call-in, the most-rehearsed behaviour of the day. And did Jess's
patient-by-patient pipeline handover happen (£70,622 across 116 open plans)?
*Sources: [REV-CALL] for the hire; [CU] for the pipeline value.*

**7. Honest status of the August quick wins**, above all the backlog calling: 250
patients with £472,514 presented and never answered. The day opens with a
no-blame status check, and John needs the real picture before asking the room.
*Sources: [PLAN] for the quick-wins list; [CU] for the backlog figures.*

**8. Is the VoiceStack routing change scoped?** New-patient calls to a TCO line
with fall-through to reception. It gates the entire fortnight-after beat; if it
needs a vendor conversation, that starts now.
*Source: [REV-CALL].*

**9. Did the contracts and roles conversation happen, and how did it land?**
This is the biggest unknown for the day's design: an excited team gets the
standard agenda, a nervous one gets more security framing, more time for
questions, and anonymous question cards at the breaks.
*Source: [EMAIL].*
